# Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for security vulnerabilities.

Instead, email the maintainers (replace with your team contact) or use GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting (Security tab → "Report a vulnerability").

Please include:

- A description of the issue and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce or a proof of concept.
- Affected component (backend / frontend / infra).

We aim to acknowledge reports promptly and will coordinate a fix and disclosure
timeline with you.
